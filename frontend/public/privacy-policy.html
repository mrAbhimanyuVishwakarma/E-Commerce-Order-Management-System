Privacy Policy

Introduction

At AxeDrobe, we value your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy outlines the types of data we collect, how we use it, and the measures we take to keep it safe.

We may collect the following information when you visit our site or make a purchase:

  • Contact information (e.g., name, email address, shipping address, phone number).
  • Payment information (processed securely through our payment gateways).
  • Browsing data (e.g., IP address, browser type, pages visited).

How We Use Your Information

Your information is used to provide you with the best possible service, including:

  • Processing and fulfilling your orders.
  • Communicating with you about your order status, promotions, and news.
  • Improving our website, product offerings, and customer experience.

Data Security

We implement a variety of security measures to maintain the safety of your personal information. Your data is contained behind secured networks and is only accessible by a limited number of persons who have special access rights to such systems.
